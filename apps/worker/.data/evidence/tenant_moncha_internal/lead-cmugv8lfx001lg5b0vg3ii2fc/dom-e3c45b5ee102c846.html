Our Services

  • General Dentistry

    Polish teeth, fill cavities or remove wisdom teeth. Explore painless options.

Your Invisalign® Journey

1

First Consultation at $50

  • Registration of interest
  • Dental examination of teeth, bite, and facial profile
  • Initial consultation to assess suitability for Invisalign® or other treatment options
    *Consultation fee waived if you proceed with treatment
2

Digital Scans & Records (included in Stage 1 sign-on)

  • iTero 3D digital scan with single-use sleeve
  • Extra-oral photos
  • Radiographs if required such as panoramic X-ray or lateral ceph
3

Treatment Planning $2,000 to $2,500

  • Personalised Invisalign® software plan
  • Digital simulation of your new smile
  • Laboratory preparation of aligners
4

Aligner Fitting and Care

  • Custom clear aligners created with precision mapping
  • Fitting of aligners with bonding of retention attachments
  • Care and wear instructions provided
5

Progress Reviews $500 per visit

  • Aligner changes every 2 weeks
  • Regular review visits every 6 to 8 weeks
  • Adjustments and refinements as needed
6

Completion & Retention $950

  • Final check and completion of Invisalign® treatment
  • Upper and lower retainers provided to maintain your new smile
  • Guidance for long-term oral health and care

Note: Non-compliance with aligner wear or retainer use may result in additional costs for refinements or retreatment.
