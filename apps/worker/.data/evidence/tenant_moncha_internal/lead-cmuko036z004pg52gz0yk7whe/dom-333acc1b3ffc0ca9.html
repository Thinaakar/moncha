What We Do

Our Services

Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy

Need relief from muscle, bone, or joint pain? We target the root cause with tailored therapy plans combining treatment and exercise.

Sports Physiotherapy

Sports Physiotherapy caters to athletes and individuals looking to recover from injuries, boost performance, and avoid future setbacks.

Post Operative Rehabilitation

Recovering from surgery? Our rehab program helps restore strength and mobility safely, ensuring a smooth return to daily life.

Pilates

Our sessions blend fitness and rehab to develop core strength, flexibility, and better posture—all in a low-impact, safe environment.

Dry Needling

Dry Needling targets areas in tight muscles to reduce pain and improve mobility—helpful when other therapies aren't fully effective.

Sports Massage

Enhance performance, speed up recovery, and prevent injuries with Sports Massage that targets specific muscle groups and relieves tension.

Gym Rehabilitation Programs

Rebuild strength and confidence after an injury or setback? Our Gym Rehabilitation Programs combine clinical expertise with guided workouts.

Telehealth Physio Consults

Secure video sessions with your physiotherapist for full assessments or quick check-ins between clinic visits—so your rehab stays on track even when you cannot get to the clinic.

Shockwave Therapy

Uses high-energy sound waves to accelerate the healing of stubborn tendon or soft tissue injuries non-invasively and effectively.

A Glance at

Our Expertise

Find the Pattern

We look for what is driving the problem, not only where you feel it. Triggers, movement under load, what you have started avoiding, and the demands of sport, work, or daily life all matter. The aim is a clear working picture of what is likely contributing, and what is not.

Build ACC

ACC means Agency, Clarity, Confidence. It is the shift from being “treated” to being involved. We explain what we see in plain language, set priorities, and make the purpose of each step obvious. You leave knowing what to focus on, and why.

Progress with a Plan

Progress is built, not hoped for. We develop strength, capacity, and tolerance around your goals, then adjust as you respond. Each session should connect to the next, with clear next steps you can carry into the week.
