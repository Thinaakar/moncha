Find the right treatment for your dental concern

Learn more about common dental concerns and the treatments that may help address them.

Toothache

Tooth pain can feel dull, sharp, or throbbing. It may come and go, or become worse when chewing.

Possible treatment options
  • Dental check-up and X-ray
  • Tooth filling
  • Root canal treatment
  • Extraction if the tooth cannot be saved
Cracked Tooth

A cracked tooth may be hard to spot. Pain often appears when biting, chewing, or drinking something cold.

Possible treatment options
  • Dental assessment
  • Tooth bonding or filling
  • Dental crown
  • Root canal if the nerve is affected
Crooked Teeth

Teeth that are crowded, spaced, or uneven may affect cleaning, chewing, and bite comfort.

Possible treatment options
  • Braces
  • Invisalign clear aligners
  • Retainers after treatment
Tooth Sensitivity

Sensitivity may feel like a quick sharp pain when taking cold, hot, sweet, or acidic food and drinks.

Possible treatment options
  • Dental check-up
  • Fluoride care
  • Tooth filling
  • Gum treatment if needed
Bleeding Gums

Gums that bleed during brushing or flossing may be a sign of inflammation around the teeth.

Possible treatment options
  • Scaling and polishing
  • Deep cleaning or root planing
  • Gum treatment for advanced cases
Swollen Gums

Swelling near a tooth or around the gums may point to infection, trapped food, or gum irritation.

Possible treatment options
  • Dental cleaning
  • Gum treatment
  • Wisdom tooth assessment
  • Medication only when clinically needed
Missing Tooth

A missing tooth can affect chewing, speech, and how nearby teeth sit over time.

Possible treatment options
  • Dental implant
  • Dental bridge
  • Partial denture
Stained Teeth

Teeth may look yellow, dull, or uneven in colour even with regular brushing.

Possible treatment options
  • Scaling and polishing
  • Teeth whitening
  • Veneers for deeper colour concerns
Chipped Tooth

A chip may be small, but it can still cause rough edges, sensitivity, or cosmetic concern.

Possible treatment options
  • Composite bonding
  • Dental filling
  • Veneers
  • Crown for larger damage
Jaw or Bite Pain

Jaw soreness, clicking, or pain when chewing may be linked to the bite or jaw joint.

Possible treatment options
  • Bite assessment
  • Night guard
  • Orthodontic assessment
  • Restorative treatment if teeth are worn

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a dentist visit cost in Singapore?

A dental consultation at Casa Dental starts from $20 for a short consultation. Scaling and polishing starts from $80. All prices are subject to GST. An additional consumables charge of $5 applies per visit. CHAS, Pioneer Generation, and Merdeka Generation cardholders are eligible for additional subsidies at all 9 branches.

Can I use Medisave at a dental clinic in Singapore?

Yes. Medisave can be used for eligible surgical procedures at all Casa Dental branches, including dental implant surgery (from $2,800), wisdom tooth surgery (from $720), bone graft and sinus lift surgery (from $950), and certain minor oral surgeries (from $300). All prices are subject to GST. Our team can walk you through exactly how much you can claim before your appointment.

Does Casa Dental offer instalment payment plans?

Yes. 0% instalment plans are available for selected treatments including Invisalign (from $5,500) and dental implants (from $2,800). All prices are subject to GST. Our team will advise you on eligible payment options during your consultation.

Is Casa Dental a good dental clinic in Singapore?

Casa Dental has been recognised as a Black Diamond Invisalign provider since 2024, one of the highest provider tiers awarded in Singapore, and holds over 8,000 five-star Google reviews across all 9 branches. Every branch is led by a full-time dentist who attends Continuing Dental Education at more than double the annual requirement set by the Singapore Dental Council.

How experienced are the dentists at Casa Dental?

Every Casa Dental branch is led by a full-time, permanent dentist who is also a clinic partner. Our doctors attend Continuing Dental Education sessions at more than double the requirement set by the Singapore Dental Council, and several hold advanced training in implant dentistry and orthodontics.

What safety and infection control measures are in place?

Every Casa Dental branch follows strict infection control protocols. An additional consumables charge of $5 applies per visit, covering sterilisation, single-use instruments, and hygiene standards maintained across all 9 locations.

Is there a dental clinic open on Sunday in Singapore?

Yes. Several Casa Dental branches are open on Sundays and weekends across our 9 locations in Ang Mo Kio, Admiralty, Bedok, Boon Keng, Bukit Batok, Clementi, Holland, Tengah, and Woodlands. Check the branch page nearest to you for exact opening hours.

What dental services are available at Casa Dental?

Casa Dental offers a full range of dental treatments across all 9 branches including dental implants, Invisalign, braces, veneers, wisdom tooth extraction, root canal treatment, scaling and polishing, teeth whitening, gum treatment, dental crowns and bridges, and emergency dentistry. All prices are subject to GST. Medisave-claimable procedures and 0% instalment plans are available at every location.

Which dental clinic is nearest to me in Singapore?

Casa Dental operates 9 branches across Singapore covering Ang Mo Kio, Admiralty, Bedok, Boon Keng, Bukit Batok, Clementi, Holland, Tengah, and Woodlands. Head to our contact page to find the branch nearest to you with full address, opening hours, and a direct WhatsApp link.

How do I book a dentist appointment in Singapore?

You can book an appointment at any Casa Dental branch by filling out our online booking form, calling our hotline at +65 6662 8180, or messaging any branch directly via WhatsApp. Please note that submitting the online form does not automatically confirm your appointment. Our team will contact you by phone to confirm.

How long does a dental appointment typically take?

A routine consultation and scaling and polishing typically takes 30 to 45 minutes. More complex procedures such as dental implant surgery or wisdom tooth surgery may take longer depending on the case. Our team will give you a time estimate when confirming your appointment.

How often should I go to the dentist?

Most patients benefit from a dental visit every 6 months for a routine checkup and scaling and polishing (from $80). Patients with active dental concerns, ongoing orthodontic treatment, or higher risk of gum disease may need to visit more frequently. Your dentist at Casa Dental will recommend the right schedule for your situation.

Can I use the CHAS card at Casa Dental?

Yes. CHAS, Pioneer Generation, and Merdeka Generation cards are accepted at all 9 Casa Dental branches. These schemes provide subsidies on a range of dental treatments including consultations, scaling and polishing, fillings, extractions, and root canal treatment. Bring your card to your appointment and our team will process the subsidy for you. Please note CHAS does not cover dental implants. Medisave applies for implant procedures.

What can I expect during a routine dental checkup?

A routine checkup at Casa Dental typically includes a clinical examination of your teeth, gums, and oral tissues, followed by scaling and polishing to remove plaque and tartar buildup. Your dentist may also take X-rays (from $30 per bitewing) if needed and will discuss any treatment recommendations with you before proceeding.
