Why 1728 Dental Practice?

1728 Dental Practice offers comprehensive, affordable care across Singapore — including dental implants, wisdom tooth surgery, Invisalign clear aligners, and urgent dental emergencies. We also provide aesthetic dentistry and long-term solutions like dentures and crown & bridges.

Our Approach

From root canal treatment to gum therapy, every procedure is delivered gently, with transparent pricing and trusted expertise.

Our Technology

Modern equipment and digital imaging power every treatment, including wisdom tooth extraction, gum treatment, full smile restorations with crowns, bridges, or implants.

Three clinics located beside MRT stations for fast access to general dentistry and urgent care.
