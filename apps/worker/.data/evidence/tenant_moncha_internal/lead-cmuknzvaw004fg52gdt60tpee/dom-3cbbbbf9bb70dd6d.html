When to See a Physiotherapist

HelloPhysio’s Singapore physiotherapy clinics treat patients with acute or chronic pain, stiffness, weakness or reduced movement that affects everyday activities, work, exercise or sport. Back and neck pain, for example, can limit mobility, concentration and your ability to work. Physiotherapy can also support pain management and may help reduce the risk of some recurring injuries.

Back and Spine

Conditions include back pain, lower back pain, sciatica, herniated discs, lumbar spondylosis and scoliosis. Patients with back and neck pain may also have stiffness or reduced movements.

Sports Injuries

Sports physiotherapy services support sports injury management for sprains, muscle or tendon tears, overuse injuries and other musculoskeletal injuries. For patients returning to sporting activities, treatment may include targeted exercises and strength training to help prevent future injuries.

Neck, Shoulder and Upper Limb

Neck pain, shoulder pain, shoulder injuries, frozen shoulder, rotator cuff injuries, shoulder impingement, tennis elbow, and hand or wrist conditions.

Hip and Knee

Hip pain, hip tendinopathy, knee pain, ACL injuries, meniscus injuries, runner’s knee, patellar tendinopathy and knee osteoarthritis.

Foot and Ankle

Foot pain, ankle pain, ankle sprains, chronic ankle instability, Achilles tendinopathy, plantar fasciitis, peroneal tendon problems, heel pain and post-fracture stiffness.

Post-Operative Rehabilitation

Post-surgical physiotherapy supports staged recovery of movement, strength and function. Patients may receive this treatment after ACL reconstruction, meniscus or rotator cuff repair, ankle and foot surgery, fracture fixation, and hip or knee replacement.

Older Adults, Balance and Bone Health

Physiotherapy services for older adults may include strength, balance, gait and mobility training. For patients with osteoporosis or age-related bone loss, targeted exercises may improve posture and strength while addressing modifiable fall risks.

Your Physiotherapy Assessment

Process

  1. Discussion
    The clinician asks about your symptoms, health history, daily activities and goals.
  2. Assessment
    The clinician assesses relevant movement, strength, joint mobility, balance, gait, and nerve function.
  3. Explanation
    The clinician explains the findings in clear language and discusses suitable options, expected progression and any need for medical assessment.
  4. Treatment plan
    Physiotherapy treatment, advice and prescribed exercises may begin when appropriate. The clinician reviews patients’ progress using measures relevant to their condition and goals.

 

Physiotherapy Treatment Plan

A physiotherapy treatment plan may include education, activity and load management, prescribed exercises, progressive strengthening, manual therapy or Clinical Pilates. Pain management may combine movement and exercise to reduce pain and improve mobility. Individualized treatments are adjusted to your symptoms, function and recovery needs. Clear communication helps keep the plan suited to your goals.

About Physiotherapy in Singapore

What is physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy is a health profession focused on movement, physical function and rehabilitation. It can help manage pain, improve mobility and restore function affected by injury, illness, disability or surgery.

Depending on their training and work setting, physiotherapists can provide musculoskeletal or neurological rehabilitation.

HelloPhysio’s services focus primarily on musculoskeletal conditions, sports injuries and post-surgical rehabilitation.
 

What is a physiotherapist?

A physiotherapist is an allied health professional trained to assess and manage problems affecting movement and physical function. These may involve muscles, bones, joints, tendons, ligaments or nerves.
 

What does a physiotherapist do?

A physiotherapist may:

  • Review your symptoms, medical history, activities and goals.
  • Assess your movement, joint mobility, strength, balance, gait and physical function.
  • Explain factors that may be contributing to your pain or limitation.
  • Prescribe exercises and guide progressive strengthening.
  • Use manual therapy or selected adjunct treatments when appropriate.
  • Plan a safe return to daily activities, work, exercise or sport.
  • Recommend medical assessments or imagings when the findings require it.

Physiotherapist qualifications in Singapore

Physiotherapists in Singapore are regulated by the Allied Health Professions Council. They must be registered and hold a valid Practising Certificate to practice in Singapore. Each and every physiotherapist at HelloPhysio is AHPC-registered. 

Physiotherapists may complete postgraduate education or further training in specific practice areas. When choosing a physiotherapist, consider their registration, qualifications, experience with your condition, communication style and treatment approach.

Clinic location and operating hours may also affect how consistently you can attend your regular sessions.

Physiotherapy FAQs

How long is a physiotherapy appointment?

Session length depends on the service. An initial assessment is up to 45 minutes, and a follow-up session is 30 to 45 minutes.

What should I wear to a physiotherapy appointment?

Wear comfortable clothing that lets the clinician assess the affected area and the relevant movement. Shorts and a T-shirt suit many assessments. Your physio will explain if you need different clothing.

Will I receive exercises to do at home?

When appropriate, most patients will receive exercises based on their assessments, stages of recovery and their goals. The program may be adjusted as the individual’s symptoms and functions change.

Which HelloPhysio clinic should I attend?

Choose the location that is most convenient for you. HelloPhysio has physiotherapy clinics at Wisma Atria near the Orchard MRT station on Orchard Road and Raffles Hotel Arcade near the City Hall MRT station on North Bridge Road.

Why is physiotherapy important before surgery?

Physiotherapy before surgery strengthens muscles, improves joint mobility, and enhances overall conditioning, helping shorten recovery and improve outcomes.
