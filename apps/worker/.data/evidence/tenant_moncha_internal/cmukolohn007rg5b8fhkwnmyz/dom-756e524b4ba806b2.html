    FAQs

    How much does a dental appointment cost?

    Fees depend on the treatment and individual clinical findings. The clinic will explain what is involved and indicative fees during a consultation.

    Is dental care expensive in Singapore?

    Dental care costs vary. Preventive treatments like scaling are more affordable, while advanced procedures like implants or braces are costlier due to complexity. We have transparent pricing and various payment options for different budgets.

    How can I get a subsidy for dental care?

    Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents can get dental subsidies through CHAS, MediSave and Baby Bonus. These can offset costs for treatments like extractions, dentures and root canals. Eligibility and subsidy amounts depend on the scheme and treatment.

    Can I use the CHAS card for dental procedures?

    Absolutely. If you’re a CHAS, Pioneer Generation or Merdeka Generation cardholder, you’re eligible for subsidies on treatments like fillings, extractions and root canals at participating clinics. Just show us your card and NRIC during your visit.

    How do I book a dentist appointment in Singapore?

    Booking is straightforward:

    • Please contact the clinic directly by phone or email for appointment enquiries.
    • Email: Send your details to hello@nuffielddental.com.sg.
    • Phone: Call us at +65 6833 4353.

    Our team will assist you in scheduling a convenient time.

    Can I use MediSave for dental procedures?

    MediSave may be used to offset part of the cost of certain surgical dental procedures, such as wisdom tooth extractions and dental implants, within the limits set by the Ministry of Health. The dentist will advise what is applicable to your case.

    What safety measures are in place at Nuffield Dental?

    Your safety is our priority. We adhere to strict sterilisation protocols, use advanced equipment like electric motor-driven surgical handpieces to minimise contamination, and thoroughly assess medical histories to ensure safe and effective treatments.

    How experienced are the dental surgeons at Nuffield Dental?

    Our dental surgeons have received training from institutions in Singapore, Australia, the UK, and the US. Their clinical experience includes areas such as implant dentistry, orthodontic procedures, and oral surgery.

    How often should you go to the dentist?

    It's recommended to visit the dentist every six months for routine dental check-ups and cleanings. However, individuals with specific dental concerns may require more frequent visits.

    What can I expect during a routine dental check-up?

    A standard check-up includes:

    • Comprehensive oral examination
    • Professional cleaning (scaling and polishing)
    • X-rays if necessary
    • Personalised oral health advice

    Our goal is to ensure your comfort and address any concerns you may have.

    How long does a dental appointment typically take?

    Appointment durations vary:

    • Routine check-up: 30–45 minutes
    • Fillings: 30–60 minutes
    • Root canals or implants: 1–2 hours or multiple sessions

    We'll provide a time estimate during your consultation.

    What post-treatment dental care instructions should I follow?

    Post-treatment care is crucial. We'll provide specific instructions based on your procedure, which may include:

    • Dietary recommendations
    • Oral hygiene practices
    • Activity restrictions

    Following these guidelines ensures optimal recovery.
