Get directions

Our Orchard clinic is our flagship location, on Orchard Road at Orchard Towers in District 9, a few minutes from Orchard MRT. It offers the full range of physiotherapy and hand therapy services and is a convenient base for patients working or living around Tanglin, River Valley, and Newton.

Getting to Phoenix Rehab ORCHARD

  • Orchard Towers located at the junction with Claymore Road, offering both commercial (office/retail/church/F&B/services) in a prime District 9 location, well-connected by Orchard MRT and numerous bus routes, surrounded by malls like ION Orchard, Forum The Shopping Mall, and Palais Renaissance. 
  • We're located at front Orchard Towers, facing Orchard road and there is a stairway entrance at the front and the back.
  • WHEELCHAIR ACCESS / RAMP: Drive/taxi to the parking lot in the BACK Orchard Towers, and go to 5th floor which has a linkbridge to Front Orchard Towers. Take the lift just uphead beyond the linkbridge to level 12.
Get directions

By car

Parking (important: entrance is not the main front)

  • The carpark entrance is at the rear residential block, accessed from the side street (not the main Orchard Road entrance). Look for the ramp leading into the multi-storey carpark.

  • Orchard Towers is accessible via Orchard Road / Claymore Road / Claymore Drive (so if Orchard Road is jammed, approach via Claymore side)

By bus

By bus to Orchard Towers (400 Orchard Rd)

  1. Alight at: Delfi Orchard (Bus Stop 09169)
    Buses (common ones): 7, 36, 36B, 77, 105, 106, 111, 123, 132, 174, 174e, 502, 502A

  2. Walk to Orchard Towers (about 1–3 min):
    From the bus stop, turn right and walk along the pathway, then use the zebra crossing to cross over. You’ll see Orchard Towers right after.

  3. Inside the building: take the lift to Level 12, #12-12.

Alternative stop (also very near): Royal Thai Embassy (Bus Stop 09179), then walk over

By train / MRT

MRT: Alight at Orchard MRT (NS22/TE14). Walk 5–8 mins to Orchard Towers.

Alight at Orchard MRT → exit towards ION/Orchard Road → walk to Orchard Towers along Orchard Road → lift to Level 12, #12-12.

By taxi / hailing apps

Drive/Grab: Search “Orchard Towers, 400 Orchard Road”. Park in the building (subject to availability), then lift to Level 12, #12-12.

HAND OCCUPATIONAL THERAPY
One of the few clinics in Singapore with trained, experienced specialist hand therapists.

Hand therapy is its own discipline — with its own training, and its own tools

  • In Singapore it’s practiced 99.9% by occupational therapists, and majority inside the public hospitals. Our hand occupational therapists are trained in those same hospital hand occupational therapy departments — which is the only place in Singapore you can learn it properly…no other professions in Singapore has the same finger, hand, wrist, forearm and elbow injury, pain and post-surgery training or exposure other than hand occupational therapists.
  • They also fabricate on-the-spot custom thermoplastic splints, which we make in-house for fingers, hand, wrist, forearm and elbows (shoulders too).
  • For post-operative hand and wrist recovery, hand occupational therapy is a must. The difference in movement, grip strength and stamina between patients who do hand therapy and patients who don’t can be significant.
  • That’s why hand therapy is rarely available in private clinics, and sometimes other profession attempt to treat the upper limb generally…but the finger, hand all the way to elbow isn’t the same as large joints and structures – they’re a lot more intricacies and delicate structures that may intertwine and glide in a very careful manner that requires precision, careful, skilled and experienced management.
