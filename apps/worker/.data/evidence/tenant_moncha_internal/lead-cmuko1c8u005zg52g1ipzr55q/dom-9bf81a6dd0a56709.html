FAQs

Are physical therapy and physiotherapy the same, and what is the main purpose of physiotherapy?

Yes, physical therapy and physiotherapy are just interchangeable names. The main purpose of physiotherapy is to identify movement disorders, restore movement, reduce pain, and improve overall function. At River Physio, we provide evidence-based treatments tailored to each patient’s condition and lifestyle.

Where can I find a trusted physiotherapy clinic in Singapore?

River Physio has multiple branches across Singapore, including Dempsey, Robinson, Orchard, and Raffles, making it easy to access expert care wherever you are located.

How do I choose the right physiotherapy clinic?

When choosing a physiotherapy clinic, consider accessibility, therapist expertise, available services, and patient reviews. River Physio’s multiple locations make it simple to find the right clinic for your needs.

What services does your physiotherapy clinic in Singapore provide?

Our physiotherapy clinic in Singapore offers treatment for sports injuries, post-surgery rehabilitation, chronic pain management, elderly mobility support, and more.

Is River Physio suitable for first-time patients looking for a physio clinic?

Yes, River Physio is an excellent choice if you’re visiting a physioclinic in Singapore for the first time. We provide a clear assessment, explain your condition, and create a step-by-step recovery plan.

How do I book an appointment?

You can easily schedule a session at any of River Physio’s clinics. With locations in Dempsey, Robinson, Orchard, and Raffles, booking is convenient. To get started, simply visit our contact page.

What can I expect during a physiotherapy session?

During a physiotherapy session, your therapist will first assess your condition, discuss your goals, and explain the treatment plan. Sessions may include hands-on techniques, guided exercises, and advice on how to manage your recovery at home.

How do I find the right physiotherapist?

The right physiotherapist should have recognised qualifications, relevant experience, and a treatment style that fits your needs. At River Physio, our team of specialists ensures you receive personalised care from start to finish.

Which patients need physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy is recommended for patients recovering from injuries, post-surgery rehabilitation, chronic pain management, sports performance, and mobility issues. It also benefits seniors and individuals with neurological or musculoskeletal conditions.

What are the benefits of physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy helps reduce pain, restore mobility, improve strength, prevent re-injury, and enhance overall quality of life. Whether you’re managing a long-term condition or recovering from surgery, treatment at our physiotherapy centre in Singapore supports long-term wellness.
