Physiotherapy Raffles Place Clinic

Core Concepts physiotherapy Singapore has multiple clinics, one of our locations is Raffles Place located in the central business district of Singapore.

Our friendly customer care consultants and our dedicated team of specialist physiotherapists, sports massage therapists, and sports trainers will help address your different and unique musculoskeletal conditions.

We also remain dedicated to getting to the root cause of your problems, in turn assuring the long-term effectiveness of our treatments for all our clients. Here at Core Concepts, we’re about overcoming barriers and expanding human potential. We’re about enriching the lives of our clients and inspiring them to get back to their best.

We adopt a central appointment and enquiry system, making it easier for you to book your next appointment at any one of our locations.
