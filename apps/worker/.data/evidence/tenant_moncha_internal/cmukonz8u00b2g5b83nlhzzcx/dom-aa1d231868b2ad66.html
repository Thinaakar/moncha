Our Dental Services

Crowns and Bridges

Dental crowns are caps that are placed over teeth and kept in place with dental adhesive or cement to protect broken teeth.

View Treatment

Braces

Braces help align and straighten your teeth over a period of time and are a safe and reliable way of doing so.

View Treatment

Dentures

Dentures are prosthetic teeth made to replace missing teeth. Dentures are removable and are a quick fix for missing teeth

View Treatment

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry improves the colour, shape, and alignment of your teeth, such as teeth whitening, teeth straightening, and veneers.

View Treatment

Invisalign®

Invisalign is the modern way to straighten your teeth. Invisalign gently shifts your teeth to create the aesthetically perfect smile.

View Treatment

Wisdom Tooth Surgery

Wisdom tooth surgery is a minor surgical procedure that aims to remove your impacted wisdom tooth which is misaligned.

View Treatment

Dental Implants

Dental implants are modern, non-removable tooth replacements. They provide support to secure a crown and bridge firmly in place.

View Treatment

Cavity Fillings

Fillings are used to treat cavities and to repair cracked or broken teeth. They are used to fill the area on the tooth where it is lacking

View Treatment

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal treatment involves the removal of the pulp tissues from the tooth in the event that it gets infected or inflamed.

Our Location

Garden Dental Clinic is strategically located to offer the ultimate convenience for anyone seeking a dentist in Bukit Timah. We are positioned directly inside Botanic Gardens MRT (Downtown Line, Exit B), providing sheltered, direct access for the residents, families, and professionals that call this prime district home.

Our location advantage means:

 

Effortless Commutes: Easily accessible from anywhere along the Bukit Timah and Dunearn Road corridors.

 

Lifestyle Integration: Perfect for appointments before work, after school drop-offs, or during a visit to the nearby Botanic Gardens or Cluny Court.

 

A Unique Environment: We are not just another dental clinic; we are a health sanctuary inspired by the serenity of our surroundings, offering a calm and positive experience every time.
