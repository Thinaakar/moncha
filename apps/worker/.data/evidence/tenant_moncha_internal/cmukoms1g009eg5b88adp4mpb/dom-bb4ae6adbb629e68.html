About

LQ Dental is a fully equipped dental centre providing comprehensive care since 1990. What began in Raffles Place as a small team of four surgeons has grown into a comprehensive practice at Novena Medical Center, with over 10 dental professionals including general practitioners, oral surgeons, and a periodontist.

Our philosophy is simple: we treat the patient, not just the tooth. With digital technology and a patient-oriented approach, we offer a full spectrum of services, with particular focus in dental implants, orthodontics, aesthetic dentistry, oral surgery, preventive care, endodontics, and gum treatment.
