Our Services

Tooth Fillings

A dental surgeon will carefully remove the decayed material and restore the tooth’s function using aesthetic white fillings maintaining both function and appearance.

Fluoride Treatment

Involves the topical application of fluoride to the tooth to improve resistance to dental decay and strengthen weakened tooth enamel and much more.

Tooth Extractions

Also known as exodontia or exodontics, is the process of removing a tooth from its socket (dental alveolus) in the alveolar bone. A surgical dentist at Dental Essence will always have a patient’s comfort and well-being as their primary objective when carrying out these procedures.

Root Canal Treatment

A root canal procedure involves the removal of unhealthy or infected pulp tissue within the tooth, helping to alleviate pain and restore dental health.

CAD CAM

Computer-aided design and manufacture (CAD/CAM) facilitate rapid and accurate communication with dental laboratories, allowing patients to better understand their treatment before it begins.

Inlays and Onlays

Inlays and Onlays are custom restorations crafted in a dental lab and used to restore teeth. These treatments typically require less preparation than crowns, allowing for the preservation of more natural tooth structure while still providing strength and aesthetic appeal.

Dental Bridge

An effective solution for replacing missing teeth. Crowns are placed on the teeth adjacent to the gap, supporting a false tooth to improve functionality and aesthetics.

Tooth Fillings

Involves removing decayed material from a tooth and restoring its function using aesthetic white filling materials. 

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns can be used to add support to a tooth that is structurally compromised. The white materials used yield favourable aesthetic results.

Teeth Bonding

Bonding can be used to make adjustments to the edge of teeth, giving you a beautiful smile by restoringe its worn edges. This often requires minimal preparation.

Smile Makeover

A Smile Makeover is an umbrella term used to describe the transformation of a smile that might be misshapen, discoloured or crooked, enhancing it to align with the patient’s facial aesthetics.

Whitening

Using chemicals and light to improve the colour of the tooth, this procedure offers a minimally invasive way to improve the appearance of the smile.

Dental Veneers

Dental veneers are thin layers of porcelain or plastic placed over the visible surfaces of teeth to improve their shape and colour. This often requires preparation of the tooth structure beforehand.

Oral Surgery

Oral surgery encompasses a wide range of procedures, from simple tooth extractions to complex orthognathic surgery, which involves surgically repositioning the jaws in relation to the head and neck.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a highly sought-after treatment of choice as they closely replicate natural dental structures. A titanium screw is placed into the jawbone by a specialist oral surgeon, providing a durable and functional solution.

Myobrace

Myobrace is a no-braces pre-orthodontic approach to help straighten your teeth and jaws. This treatment addresses the poor oral habits that can contribute to crooked teeth.

Braces

Metal or ceramic brackets are cemented onto the teeth and a wire gradually aligns them as it straightens.

Invisalign

They function the same as fixed braces except that they are designed to be an invisible and removable orthodontic treatment method that uses sequential clear aligners that fit over your teeth.

OneSmile

Similar to Invisalign, One Smile utilises AI function to help ensure consistency and compliance with wearing your aligners.
