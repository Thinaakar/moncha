The Dental Designs Difference

Welcome to Dental Designs Clinic Singapore, a haven where compassionate care and exceptional dentistry converge. We're on a mission to offer dentistry that combines precision with comfort.

We take the time to listen to your concerns, understand your goals, and tailor our approach to suit your needs. Using modern innovations in dental technology, we strive for precision and excellence in every treatment we provide. Let us help you achieve a smile that you deserve, that will give you the confidence to be your best.

Dr Nicholas Ng

Dentist

“Providing quality care for my patients”

Dr Nicholas Ng received his Bachelor of Dental Surgery from the National University of Singapore. As a general dentist, Dr Nicholas is passionate about restoring both form and function to his patients’ smiles. Patients feel both care and competence in the way that he practices dentistry.

Over the years, he has accumulated experience in treating a myriad of dental problems, and ardently pursues helping his patients with a holistic view on their oral health. 

Dr Nicholas counts it a privilege to have had many opportunities to help patients resolve their dental pain and stabilise poor oral conditions. He has worked at secondary school dental services, polyclinics, Khoo Teck Puat Hospital and also at National Dental Centre, where he dedicated a year to the Endodontics department, focused on treating patients with dental pain. Hence, he is often recommended when patients are experiencing toothaches.
