Before your first appointment

Do I need a doctor’s referral?+

In many situations you can book a physiotherapy assessment directly. A referral or medical instructions may still be needed for insurance, workplace claims, recent surgery, or particular medical conditions. Contact the clinic if you are unsure.

What should I wear?+

Wear comfortable clothing that allows movement and access to the area being assessed. Bring shorts or exercise wear if that makes the assessment easier.

How long is an appointment?+

The first physiotherapy session is typically 45 minutes, so there is time for assessment, explanation, and a first plan. Follow-up sessions are usually 30 minutes. If several areas need attention, a longer session can be discussed beforehand.

What are your physiotherapy session fees?+

Published fees before GST are: First / Re-Assessment Session: S$180 (45 minutes) Subsequent Session: S$150 to S$180 (30 to 45 minutes) Lymphatic Therapy: S$120 (30 minutes) Lymphatic Therapy: S$180 (1 hour) Shockwave Therapy: S$250 Accepted payment methods: cash, NETS, Visa, Mastercard, and AMEX. Senior / Principal therapist request surcharge: additional S$30 to S$60. Re-assessment may be required after a gap of more than 3 months, as your condition may have changed.
